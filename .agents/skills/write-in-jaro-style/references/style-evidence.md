# Style Evidence

This guide was derived from all 20 posts published on `jarosays.blog` between 2017 and 2026, not from a small sample. More weight was given to the recent posts because they better reflect Jaro's current voice.

## Recurring patterns

- Concrete personal observations often open the door to a larger argument.
- Abstract ideas are made tangible through familiar comparisons: vehicles, food, diagrams, games, clothing, boats, and everyday technology.
- Short paragraphs and occasional sentence fragments create conversational rhythm.
- Questions are used as hinges in the reasoning.
- Strong claims are paired with caveats, counterexamples, or admissions of uncertainty.
- Humour is dry, self-aware, and often placed in an aside rather than delivered as a punchline.
- Contrasts do much of the structural work.
- Local and biographical details make the writing specific without turning it into memoir for its own sake.
- Endings are usually compact and memorable, but the recent work is comfortable leaving a thought honestly unresolved.

## Evolution of the voice

The earlier writing is more expansive, sarcastic, and eager to entertain. The more recent writing is shorter, calmer, and more willing to let one observation stand without wrapping it in a grand conclusion. Default to the recent voice. Borrow the older energy selectively when the subject and audience can carry it.
